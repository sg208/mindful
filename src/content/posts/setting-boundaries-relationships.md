Many of us grew up believing that saying no or putting our needs first is selfish. In reality, boundaries are what keep relationships healthy and sustainable. Without them, we can end up exhausted, resentful, or losing ourselves in other people's expectations. Boundaries aren't walls—they're the lines that help us show up without burning out.

A boundary is simply a limit you set around what you're willing to do, accept, or give. It might be about time, energy, or how you're treated. Setting a boundary doesn't require a long explanation. A clear, kind "no" or "I'm not comfortable with that" is enough. Your limits are valid simply because they're yours.

It helps to name boundaries before you're at breaking point. People can't respect boundaries they don't know about. Communicating them clearly and early reduces conflict and confusion. Others may push back. That doesn't mean your boundary is wrong. Start with one relationship or one situation where you've been overgiving. Small steps count.
