Formal meditation is valuable, but mindfulness doesn't have to stay on the cushion. You can bring present-moment awareness to almost any activity: washing dishes, walking, eating, or even waiting in line. The idea is to notice what you're doing and what's happening in your body and mind, without being lost in autopilot. The activity becomes the practice.

Try one routine activity today and do it with full attention. Feel the water on your hands, the taste of your food, or the sensation of your feet on the ground. When your mind wanders, gently bring it back. Even one minute of fully present dishwashing or one bite of food eaten with full attention counts.

Little by little, this can change how you experience ordinary moments. Life is mostly made of "ordinary" moments. Making them more conscious can add up to a calmer, more grounded mind. The cushion and the rest of life support each other.
